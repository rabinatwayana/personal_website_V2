Living Planet Symposium 2025

  • Event Detail : https://lps25.esa.int
  • Duration : 23—27 June 2025
  • Location : Austria Center Vienna, Austria

I volunteered two days (24 & 25 June) in the Living Planet Symposium, which is the world’s premier events on Earth observation.

On 24 June, Poster Sessions Support I was responsible for following task:

  • Help authors to navigate the poster area to identify their assigned poster board.
  • Check if pins are available and ask the venue to provide more if not enough.
  • Dismantle posters still hanging at the end of each poster session as they are different each day.
  • You will be provided with tablets where to check the number of each poster.
On 25 June, Agora Sessions Support I was assigned in Agora Nexus area. I was responsible for following task:
  • Support organisers and participants to verify that all presentations are available in the folder with the session name on the presentation laptop
  • Support circulation of the microphone (in case of Q&A).
Personal Reflection :
Attending the Living Planet Symposium 2025 was an incredible and eye-opening experience for me. Listening to EO experts share their journeys, ideas, and challenges in different agora sessions made me realise how much dedication and creativity go into turning satellite data into real-world solutions. One of the highlights was visiting all the exhibition booths—collecting brochures, exploring the projects different companies and research groups are working on, and seeing how diverse and dynamic the EO community truly is. Each booth told a story of innovation, whether it was about new satellite missions, AI-driven platforms, or local applications of EO data. I discovered several innovative companies whose work really resonated with my interests:
  • FlyPixAI : Showcasing AI-powered object detection and change detection tools for satellite imagery
  • SatCHAT : Introducing a platform that makes satellite data analysis more collaborative and accessible through chat-based interfaces, lowering the barrier for non-experts to explore EO data.
  • STARSAR-X : Focusing on advanced SAR data analytics and near-real-time flood and wildfire monitoring, directly aligning with my own research interests in SAR applications.
  • MAGELIUM Artal GROUP : Presenting end-to-end EO solutions from data processing to application services, including tools for land cover mapping and environmental monitoring.
  • MindEarth : Project (A Novel GAN-based approach for Super-Resolution of Nighttime Light Imagery). Integrating AI and EO data to create tools that help understand human impact on the planet, which I found inspiring in terms of combining technology and social good.
  • SISTEMA : Super-Resolution for urban classification and change detection, utilizes cutting-edge AI techniques to enhance Sentinel-2 optical imagery, improving spatial resolution up to 3 meters.
  • Data Terra : A research e-infrastructure to access, process and combine multi-source data
The poster area left a big impression on me, especially seeing so many creative applications of Sentinel-1 SAR data for wildfire mapping and other environmental monitoring topics that align closely with my interests. It felt both exciting and comforting to see that what I’m passionate about is part of a bigger global conversation. I was amazed by the new advancements in the field of EO, especially the integration of AI and machine learning that’s transforming how we analyse data and respond to disasters and climate change impacts. It was inspiring to hear how these technologies are being applied, and also to learn about the real challenges researchers and practitioners face along the way. Overall, this symposium made me feel so motivated and re-energised. It reminded me why I chose this field in the first place: to contribute to solutions that help people and the planet. Seeing the global EO community’s work firsthand strengthened my commitment to keep learning, collaborating, and exploring new ideas to make an impact.
