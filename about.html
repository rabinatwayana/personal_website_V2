About Me

I am certified geomatics engineer with four years of professional experience in research and geospatial programming, specializing in webGIS application development. Currently, I am pursuing copernicus master’s in digital earth specializing in geodata science at Paris Lodron University Salzburg, Austria. I am Passionate about leveraging the potential of emerging geospatial technologies including geoAI, digital twin, and geospatial programming to address challenges in disaster management.

Throughout my career, I have had the privilege of working with esteemed organizations such as the GeoInformatics Center, Naxa Pvt. Ltd., and Airlift Company, participating in projects at local, provincial, national, and international levels. My contributions span diverse initiatives for respected institutions, including the University of Twente (UT) in the Netherlands, World Food Programme (WFP), World Bicycle Relief (WBF), Bhutan Construction and Transport Authority, Geodetic Survey Division of the Survey Department in Nepal, Clean-up Nepal, Changunarayan Municipality, and Kathmandu Metropolitan City. This journey has provided me with substantial expertise in WebGIS, geospatial programming, GIS, UAV technologies, remote sensing, and disaster management.
